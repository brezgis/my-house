## Furnishing

- Furniture and decor models (assets/models): Poly Haven, CC0. Textures reduced in size.
- Paintings (assets/art), public-domain scans via Wikimedia Commons: Levitan, *Golden Autumn*
  (1895); Klimt, *The Kiss*; Bruegel, *Hunters in the Snow*; Van Gogh, *Sunflowers*;
  Carl Larsson, *Blomsterfönstret*; Vermeer, *The Milkmaid*.
- Framed drawings (assets/art/plan_*.jpg): crops of Greene & Greene's 1906 sheets for the Bentz
  house, from scans held by Columbia University's Avery Library. Check the rights position
  before publishing these on the open web.
- `garden_bg` / `garden_1k.hdr`: Poly Haven `pretoria_gardens`, CC0. `bark`: Poly Haven
  `bark_brown_02`, CC0. `grass`: ambientCG `Grass004`, CC0.
- Rugs, chalkboard, screens, trees and all other furniture are drawn or built in code.

## Type and interface

- Sorts Mill Goudy (assets/fonts/goudy-regular.woff2, goudy-italic.woff2), by Barry Schwartz, a
  revival of Frederic Goudy's Goudy Oldstyle (1915). SIL Open Font License 1.1. Latin subset
  as served by Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Sorts+Mill+Goudy).
- The lantern mark, favicon, cloud-lift rule and the social card (assets/card.jpg) were drawn
  for this house.
- The library globe's map (assets/tex/globe_antique.jpg) is redrawn in an antique palette from
  NASA's Blue Marble Next Generation, topography and bathymetry, December 2004 (NASA Earth
  Observatory, R. Stöckli; public domain):
  https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/
- The moon's face, the chess rules and Fig's chess play (src/chess_rules.js) were written for this house.
  The chess set itself is the Poly Haven model listed above (CC0).
